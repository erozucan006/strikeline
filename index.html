Knowledge check · MiFID II

Levels that grow with you.

EU rules ask brokers to check that complex products fit the client. Strikeline turns that into seven short questions. Your answers set your level, and each level unlocks the strategies you have shown you understand.

LEVEL 1

Buy options

The most you can lose is the premium you pay.

  • Long call
  • Long put
  • Straddle
  • Strangle
Unlocks with 3 of the 4 basic questions right.
LEVEL 2

Spreads

You sell options too, but every one is covered by an option you buy, so the maximum loss is known up front.

  • Bull call spread
  • Bear put spread
  • Iron condor
Unlocks with the spread question right.
LEVEL 3

Uncovered selling

Selling without protection. Losses can be far larger than the premium you receive.

  • Short put
  • Short call
  • Short strangle
Unlocks with both selling questions right and real trading experience.

Guard rails

Mistakes are caught before they cost you.

Large orders need a second click

When an order puts a quarter of your cash at risk, or its loss has no limit, the first click shows the risk. Only the second one places it.

Blocked orders come with fixes

If an order is above your level, the ticket names the leg that is the problem and fixes it in one click.
